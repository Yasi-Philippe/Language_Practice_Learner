# Italian — Learning Roadmap (grammar & concepts)

The app drills **vocabulary**. This document is the other half: the **grammar and structures** to study on your own, level by level. Consult it to know *what concepts to look up* at each stage. Vocabulary is assumed (the app covers it) — this is structure only.

> Each level builds on the ones before it. Don't chase a later concept until its foundations are solid.

---

## A1 — foundations

**Goal:** introduce yourself, ask/answer simple questions, talk about everyday things in the present.

- **Verbs:** present tense (*presente indicativo*) — regular `-are / -ere / -ire` (incl. `-isc-` type: *capire, finire*); key irregulars: *essere, avere, fare, andare, stare, dare, venire, dire, uscire, potere, volere, dovere, sapere*.
- **Nouns:** gender (m/f) and plural (`-o→-i`, `-a→-e`, `-e→-i`).
- **Articles:** definite (*il, lo, l', la, i, gli, le*) and indefinite (*un, uno, una, un'*).
- **Adjectives:** agreement in gender & number; two classes (`-o/-a/-i/-e` and `-e/-i`); normal position.
- **Pronouns:** subject pronouns (*io, tu, lui/lei, noi, voi, loro*); polite *Lei*.
- **Possessives:** *il mio, la mia, i miei…* (+ the article).
- **Demonstratives:** *questo / quello*.
- **Prepositions:** simple (*di, a, da, in, con, su, per, tra/fra*) and **articulated** (*del, al, dal, nel, sul…*).
- **Questions & negation:** *chi, che/cosa, dove, quando, come, quanto, perché, quale*; negation with *non*.
- **Existence:** *c'è / ci sono*.
- **Likes:** *mi piace / mi piacciono* (basic).
- **Numbers, dates, time, days, months; quantifiers** *molto/poco/troppo* (agreeing).

## A2 — everyday past & future

**Goal:** narrate past events, describe habits, express plans and simple opinions.

- **Past — passato prossimo:** *avere/essere* + past participle; agreement with *essere*; common irregular participles (*fatto, detto, preso, letto, visto…*).
- **Past — imperfetto:** formation and use (description, habit, background, age/weather).
- **Contrast:** *passato prossimo* vs *imperfetto*.
- **Future — futuro semplice:** regular + irregular stems (*sarò, avrò, andrò, farò…*).
- **Reflexive verbs:** *mi alzo, si lavano…*.
- **Object pronouns:** direct (*lo, la, li, le, mi, ti, ci, vi*) and indirect (*gli, le, mi, ti, ci, vi*).
- **Ne** (partitive) and **ci** (place) — basics.
- **Imperative:** informal *tu/noi/voi* (*parla! andiamo! finite!*).
- **Comparatives/superlatives:** *più/meno … di/che*; *il più/meno …*; irregulars (*migliore, peggiore*).
- **Progressive:** *stare + gerundio* (*sto mangiando*).
- **Special adjectives:** *bello, buono, grande, santo* (varying forms).
- **Connectors:** *e, ma, però, perché, quindi, anche, mentre, poi*; time markers *già, non ancora, appena, sempre, mai*.
- **Time expressions:** *volerci / metterci* (how long something takes).

## B1 — moods & hypotheticals

**Goal:** express opinions, wishes, doubts; handle conditionals and more connected discourse.

- **Conditional — condizionale presente:** politeness, wishes, hypotheticals (*vorrei, potrei…*).
- **Conditional — condizionale passato:** *avrei fatto…* (also "future in the past").
- **Subjunctive — congiuntivo presente:** after opinion/doubt/emotion/necessity (*penso che, credo che, è importante che, benché…*).
- **Subjunctive — congiuntivo passato:** *che tu abbia fatto…*.
- **Past perfect — trapassato prossimo:** *avevo fatto* (an earlier past).
- **Combined pronouns:** *me lo, te lo, glielo, ce ne…*.
- **Relative pronouns:** *che, cui, il quale*.
- **Imperative:** formal *Lei*; negative imperative; imperative + pronouns (*dimmi, non lo fare*).
- **Impersonal/passivante si:** *si mangia bene, si vendono case*.
- **Conditionals — type 1 (real):** *se + presente, … presente/futuro*; intro to type 2.
- **Gerund & participle** uses; **connectors:** *comunque, inoltre, tuttavia, infatti, dunque*.
- **Reported speech (basics)** and recognizing the **passato remoto** (in reading).

## B2 — nuance, register, complex syntax

**Goal:** argue a point, understand abstract/literary text, use the full tense system correctly.

- **Subjunctive — congiuntivo imperfetto & trapassato:** *se fossi, se avessi fatto…*.
- **Conditionals — type 2 (unreal present) & type 3 (unreal past):** *se avessi tempo, verrei* / *se avessi saputo, sarei venuto*.
- **Sequence of tenses (concordanza dei tempi):** matching indicative/subjunctive across main + subordinate clauses.
- **Passato remoto:** active use and literary/narrative reading.
- **Passive voice:** *essere/venire + participio*; *andare + participio* (obligation).
- **Causative:** *fare/lasciare + infinito* (*far riparare l'auto*).
- **Reported speech (full):** tense & pronoun shifts.
- **Pronominal verbs:** *farcela, andarsene, cavarsela, metterci…*.
- **Advanced connectors & register:** formal vs informal, nominalization, long subordinate chains.

## C1 — mastery (brief)

**Goal:** flexible, precise, near-native control.

- All moods/tenses used spontaneously, incl. subjunctive in every trigger context and mixed conditionals.
- **Literary tenses** (*trapassato remoto*) recognized; stylistic register control.
- Idioms, fixed expressions, discourse markers, connotation and nuance; concise vs emphatic phrasing.
- Understanding implicit meaning, irony, and specialized/academic text.
