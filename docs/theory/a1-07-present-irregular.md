# The irregular gang

> Spanish and Italian inherited many of the same irregular verbs — sometimes with identical forms: **vengo** and **tengo** are spelled exactly like *vengo* and *tengo*.

## La regola

| | fare | andare | venire | dire | uscire |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| io | **faccio** | **vado** | **vengo** | **dico** | **esco** |
| tu | fai | vai | vieni | dici | esci |
| lui / lei | fa | va | viene | dice | esce |
| noi | facciamo | andiamo | veniamo | diciamo | usciamo |
| voi | fate | andate | venite | dite | uscite |
| loro | fanno | vanno | vengono | dicono | escono |

Also: **stare** (sto, stai, sta…), **dare** (do, dai, **dà**…), **bere** (bevo, bevi…), **rimanere** (rimango…), **tenere** (tengo, tieni…).

## Come in spagnolo

- **vengo, tengo** = *vengo, tengo*; **dico** ~ *digo*; **faccio** ~ *hago*; **vado** ~ *voy*.
- The *-go* family lives in both: **vengo, tengo, rimango, salgo**.
- **vado, vai, va, vanno** and *voy, vas, va, van* come from the same Latin verb, *vadere*.

## Diverso dallo spagnolo

- **uscire** = *salir*. And **salire** = *subir*! **Salgo le scale** = *subo las escaleras*.
- **andare** mixes two roots: **vado** but **andiamo, andate** (Spanish *ir* mixes three: *voy, iba, fui*).
- **stare** has no *e-* in front: *estoy* → **sto**.

## Occhio!

- **dà** (gives) needs its accent; **da** is the preposition (*from*).
- **uscire** changes its vowel: **esco, esci, esce, escono** but **usciamo, uscite**. Its old form was *escire*, from Latin *exire* — the **u** probably came from **uscio**, an old word for "door".

## Curiosità

Spanish loves words starting with *es-*: *estar, escuela, espada, estudiar*. Latin had *stare, schola, spatha, studere* — Spanish added an *e* to make them easier to say; Italian never did: **stare, scuola, spada, studiare**. Old Italian sometimes added an *i* instead, and one fossil survives: you still put something **per iscritto**, "in writing".

## Il trucco

A Spanish word starting with *h-* often has an Italian twin with **f-**: *hacer* → **fare**, *hijo* → **figlio**, *harina* → **farina**, *humo* → **fumo**, *hormiga* → **formica**. Latin had *f* everywhere; Castilian turned it into *h*.
