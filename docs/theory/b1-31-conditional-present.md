# "Vorrei": the most useful word in Italian

> One conditional gets you through every bar, shop and office in Italy: **vorrei**. The conditional is where Italian turns polite — and where it dreams.

## La regola

| | parlare | prendere | dormire |
|---|---|---|---|
| io | parl**erei** | prend**erei** | dorm**irei** |
| tu | parl**eresti** | prend**eresti** | dorm**iresti** |
| lui / lei | parl**erebbe** | prend**erebbe** | dorm**irebbe** |
| noi | parl**eremmo** | prend**eremmo** | dorm**iremmo** |
| voi | parl**ereste** | prend**ereste** | dorm**ireste** |
| loro | parl**erebbero** | prend**erebbero** | dorm**irebbero** |

Same short stems as the future: **sarei, avrei, andrei, farei, potrei, dovrei, saprei, vorrei, verrei, berrei, rimarrei**.

## Come in spagnolo

- The same three jobs: politeness (**vorrei** = *quisiera*), wishes (**mi piacerebbe** = *me gustaría*) and advice (**dovresti** = *deberías*).
- The same irregular stems as the future, just like *tendría, podría, saldría*: **avrei, potrei, saprei**.
- The "news" conditional exists in both: **il ministro si dimetterebbe** = *el ministro dimitiría*.

## Diverso dallo spagnolo

- Spanish has *-ía* everywhere; Italian has **-ei, -esti, -ebbe, -emmo, -este, -ebbero**.
- **noi** has a double **m**: **faremmo** (conditional) vs **faremo** (future). Hold the m.
- Ordering: *quiero un café* is fine in Spain, but **voglio un caffè** sounds bossy in Italy. Say **vorrei**.

## Occhio!

- **-are** verbs turn the **a** into **e**, as in the future: **parlerei**, never *parlarei*.
- Never put the conditional after **se**: *se vorrei* ✗ → **se volessi** (that's B2).

## Curiosità

Both conditionals were born as "infinitive + *have*", but they glued on different pasts. Spanish took the imperfect: *cantar + había → cantaría*. Tuscan took the Latin perfect, *habui*, Italian **ebbi**: *cantare + ebbi → canterei*. Old Italian poets also used the Spanish-style forms — **avria, dovria, saria** — and you still meet them in old poems and opera libretti.

## Il trucco

Future stem + **-ei**: **sarò → sarei**, **farò → farei**, **vorrò → vorrei**. Know one tense, and you know the other.
