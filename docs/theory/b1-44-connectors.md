# The words that hold a paragraph together

> **Comunque, infatti, anzi, quindi**: the connectors that make you sound fluent — plus one notorious false friend.

## La regola

| meaning | Italian | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| anyway | **comunque** | *de todas formas* |
| moreover | **inoltre** | *además* |
| however | **però, tuttavia** | *pero, sin embargo* |
| indeed | **infatti** | *de hecho, efectivamente* |
| on the contrary | **anzi** | *al contrario, es más* |
| so | **quindi, dunque, perciò** | *así que, por lo tanto* |
| since | **siccome, dato che, visto che** | *como, ya que* |
| although | **benché, sebbene** + subj. | *aunque* |
| until | **finché (non)** | *hasta que* |
| as long as | **purché** + subj. | *con tal de que* |

## Come in spagnolo

- **però** = *pero*, **mentre** = *mientras*, **dunque** ≈ *luego, pues*.
- **benché, purché, affinché** take the subjunctive, like *aunque, con tal de que, para que*.

## Diverso dallo spagnolo

- **infatti** confirms what was just said (*de hecho, efectivamente*). It never means *en realidad* — that's **in realtà**.
- **finché non** + indicative: **finché non torni** = *hasta que vuelvas*.
- Spanish *sino* → Italian **ma** (formal **bensì**): **non è rosso, ma arancione**.

## Occhio!

- **comunque** + subjunctive means "however": **comunque vada** = *pase lo que pase*. On its own it means "anyway".
- **siccome** opens the sentence, like *como*: **siccome piove, resto a casa**.

## Curiosità

**allora**, which Italians say a hundred times a day, is a fossil: Latin *ad illam horam*, "at that hour". That's why it means both "then" (time) and "so" (logic) — exactly like Spanish *entonces*.

## Il trucco

Translate *de hecho* as **infatti** and *en realidad* as **in realtà** — never the other way round.
