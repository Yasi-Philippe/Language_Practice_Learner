# C1 · Infinitive and participle constructions

> *dopo* + past infinitive: *dopo aver mangiato* (ES *después de comer*) · *per* + past infinitive = for having · same subject: *penso di aver capito* · *al* + infinitive = when · the preposition before an infinitive belongs to the verb: *decidere di, riuscire a, aiutare a, smettere di* · *da* + infinitive: *una casa da vendere*

Italian sentence with one gap (`___`) → type the missing word(s). Checked strictly: accents count, capitals don't.
Cue = the verb or word to use; empty when the gap is a preposition.
First accepted answer = primary; others are also accepted.
