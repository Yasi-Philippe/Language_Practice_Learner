# B1 · Gerund and participle clauses

> gerund = while / by / since: *studiando si impara* · past gerund: *avendo finito, essendo arrivato* · past participle as a clause: *finito il lavoro…* — it agrees with its noun · *pur* + gerund = even though

Italian sentence with one gap (`___`) → type the gerund or participle. Checked strictly: accents count, capitals don't.
Cue = the verb, and *participle* when that's the form wanted; Spanish = the meaning.
First accepted answer = primary; others are also accepted.
