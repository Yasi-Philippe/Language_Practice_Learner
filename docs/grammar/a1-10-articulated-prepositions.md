# A1 · Articulated prepositions

> *di, a, da, in, su* + article become one word: *del, al, dal, nel, sul* · *dello, allo…* · *dell', all'…* · *della, alla…* · *dei, ai…* · *degli, agli…* · *delle, alle…* (*in* → *ne-*)

Italian sentence with one gap (`___`) → type the missing form. Checked strictly: accents count, capitals don't.
Cue = preposition + article; Spanish = the meaning.
First accepted answer = primary; others are also accepted.
