# B2 · Reported speech

> reporting in the past shifts things back: present → imperfetto · passato prossimo → trapassato · future → past conditional · imperative → *di* + infinitive · *qui → lì, oggi → quel giorno, domani → il giorno dopo, ieri → il giorno prima, questo → quello* · a present reporting verb changes nothing

Italian sentence with one gap (`___`) → report what was said. Checked strictly: accents count, capitals don't.
Cue = the verb (or the word to shift); Spanish = the meaning.
First accepted answer = primary; others are also accepted.
