# A1 · Possessives

> Article + possessive: *il mio, la mia, i miei, le mie* (*tuo, suo, nostro, vostro* the same) · *loro* never changes · one family member: no article (*mia madre*), but *i miei fratelli*, *il loro padre*

Italian sentence with one gap (`___`) → type the missing form. Checked strictly: accents count, capitals don't.
Cue = the possessive (masculine singular); Spanish = the meaning.
First accepted answer = primary; others are also accepted.
