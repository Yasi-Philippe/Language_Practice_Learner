# A1 · Definite articles

> *il* + consonant · *lo* + s+consonant, z, gn, ps, y · *l'* + vowel · *la* (f.) · plural: *i* · *gli* · *le*

Italian sentence with one gap (`___`) → type the missing form. Checked strictly: accents count, capitals don't.
Cue = what to put in (empty when the sentence says enough); Spanish = the meaning.
First accepted answer = primary; others are also accepted.

| Italian | Cue | Answer (accepted) | Spanish | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
