# A2 · Ne & ci

> *ne* = of it / of them, with quantities (*ne voglio due*) · *ci* = there, or *a* + thing (*ci penso*) · *ci vuole / ci vogliono* = it takes · *metterci* = to take (someone takes time)

Cue = the verb when there is one to conjugate; Spanish = the meaning.
