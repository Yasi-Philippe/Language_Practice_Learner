# B2 · Pronominal verbs: farcela, andarsene…

> verbs with *ci, ne, la* built in, each with its own meaning: *farcela* (manage) · *andarsene* (leave) · *cavarsela* (get by) · *prendersela* (take offence) · *metterci* (take time) · *avercela con* (be cross with) · *smetterla* (stop it) · *sentirsela* (feel up to) · *intendersene* (know about)

Italian sentence with one gap (`___`) → type the conjugated verb with its pronouns. Checked strictly: accents count, capitals don't.
Cue = the verb and person; Spanish = the meaning.
First accepted answer = primary; others are also accepted.

| Italian | Cue | Answer (accepted) | Spanish | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| È difficile, ma ___. | farcela · io | ce la faccio | Es difícil, pero lo consigo. | *farcela* = conseguirlo, poder con algo |
