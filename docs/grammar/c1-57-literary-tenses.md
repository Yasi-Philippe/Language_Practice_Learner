# C1 · Literary tenses: trapassato remoto & narration

> trapassato remoto = passato remoto of *avere / essere* + participle: *ebbi finito, fu partito* · only after *dopo che, (non) appena, quando* when the main verb is in the passato remoto · ES *hube terminado* — just as rare, just as literary

Italian sentence with one gap (`___`) → type the literary tense. Checked strictly: accents count, capitals don't.
Cue = the verb (and the person when the sentence drops it); Spanish = the meaning.
First accepted answer = primary; others are also accepted.
